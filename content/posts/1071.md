Dear Tenants of the Summit:

We are your neighbors at the Summit, tenants, owners, expats and locals, all united to improve the management of the community we all share and love.
We have started an action that requires your small help to

**Contact your landlord, and ask them to vote in the coming Owners' Committee Election starting at Feb 2021**

In recent years, we all see the club house facilities for children and adults have become dilapidated and are in urgent need of proper overhaul. The lobbies were no longer as clean as before, and the service quality of the property management was getting increasingly worse.
But if we do nothing, even worse thing will happen, including:
1. The Property Management Fee will increase from current 6.5 RMB/sqm to 9.5 RMB, a 46% increase. We all need the landlords to vote NO to this motion.
2. The club house will continue to be worsen, and the service will continue to shrink because the community is running on deficits in 2019, and 2020 and years to come.
3. The current management company will renew another 5 year contract that we cannot change until 2026. We also need your landlord to vote NO to this.

It is a normal process that every five years, Property Owners gather and vote for the next Owner's Committee, and vote to decide whether to renew the contract of the management company. But previous years, since we cannot reach out to enough owners, the vote can be easily manipulated, eventually causing these strong frustrations for tenants and owners. This year, this should not happen again.

All these past and present misdeeds are in total disrespect of a proper usage of the already colossal management budget.

That's also why many landlords were very grateful when their tenant pass the information to them. Although they don't live in the community, they got frustrated that their investment value does not grow as with proper management, and bear 4 million to 15 million losses in value. They are also unhappy if their tenants tell them they may consider moving out. This is maybe the first time we have an action plan and have tens of volunteers to improve the community. They will appreciate your help to let them know the change.
Please help to pass on the information to your landlord, and ask them to add WeChat (using the QRCode below or just add huixianju99). You'll do much good to our whole community, and help stop well known dirty practices impacting our daily lives in a totally unfair fashion.
Together, we have a clear path to make our community much better for everyone of us!
