如下是租客群里面大家认为汇贤居急需解决的问题：

- 车库和转盘只应该允许住户或者接送住户的车辆。车辆必须在管理处登记。

- 没有停车位的车辆禁止进入车库和转盘

- 车库里面超过5分钟的车辆开始支付停车费

- 大楼和车库里面禁烟 - 保安，司机，业主，租客无一例外

- 助动车送货员必须把助动车停在门口并且走路送货

- 只允许住户进入，其他助动车停在小区外，无一例外。

- 所有楼使用门禁系统，门卡或密码保护住客

- 会所设施升级。提前和推迟一小时营业时间

- 东门应该开放，并使用电子门禁

It’s not that complicated:

- No cars in garage (and circle) unless there is a summit owner renter in it or getting into it. Vehicle must be registered with property management.

- All cars entering who do not have a rented/paid for parking space cars to remain out of circle and garage.

- All cars that enter the garage for more than 5 minutes must pay a minimum parking fee.

- No smoking in all building public spaces and garage - security, drivers, owners, renters.

- Scooter delivery must park at front gate and walk deliver their packages.

- Scooter parking area only for paid renters. All other scooters park outside. No exceptions.

- Security entrance systems, cards and codes must be properly used to protect all apartments and people.

- The fitness club and equipment should be upgraded (including both swimming pools) and operating hours earlier in the morning and later in the evening (even 1 hour earlier and later would make a huge difference for use and perhaps fees).

- The east gate should be made functional with electronic access.
